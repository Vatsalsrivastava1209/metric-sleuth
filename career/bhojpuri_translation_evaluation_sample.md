# Bhojpuri Translation and AI Evaluation Sample

Prepared by Vatsal Srivastava  
Target role: Bhojpuri Language Expert / AI Language Trainer
Date: Apr 2026 - Present

## Purpose

This sample demonstrates native Bhojpuri judgment for AI translation evaluation. It is part of a larger 50-example personal benchmarking dataset being built to evaluate how current foundational LLMs handle regional nuances, code-switching, and idiomatic accuracy in Bhojpuri. The 10 examples below highlight critical failure modes identified during this study.

## Examples

### 1. Everyday Future Action

English: I will come tomorrow morning.  
Natural Bhojpuri: हम काल्हु बिहान आईब।  
Literal / unnatural: मैं कल सुबह आऊंगा।  
Evaluation note: The natural version uses Bhojpuri time expression "काल्हु बिहान" and the Bhojpuri future verb form "आईब." The unnatural version is standard Hindi, not Bhojpuri.

### 2. Polite Request

English: Please send me the address.  
Natural Bhojpuri: मेहरबानी करके हमरा पता भेज दीं।  
Literal / unnatural: कृपया मुझे पता भेजें।  
Evaluation note: "हमरा" and "भेज दीं" sound more natural in Bhojpuri polite speech. The literal version is understandable but reads like Hindi.

### 3. Ongoing Action

English: Are you eating now?  
Natural Bhojpuri: का रउआ अभी खात बानी?  
Literal / unnatural: क्या आप अभी खा रहे हैं?  
Evaluation note: The natural version uses respectful Bhojpuri pronoun "रउआ" and Bhojpuri progressive form "खात बानी." The unnatural version is Hindi.

### 4. Comprehension

English: I do not understand this sentence.  
Natural Bhojpuri: हमरा ई वाक्य बुझात नइखे।  
Literal / unnatural: मैं यह वाक्य नहीं समझता।  
Evaluation note: Bhojpuri often uses "हमरा ... बुझात नइखे" for "I do not understand." The literal version transfers Hindi structure directly.

### 5. Task Completion

English: This work should be finished today.  
Natural Bhojpuri: ई काम आजे पूरा होखे के चाहीं।  
Literal / unnatural: यह काम आज पूरा होना चाहिए।  
Evaluation note: "ई काम," "आजे," and "होखे के चाहीं" preserve Bhojpuri grammar and rhythm. The literal version is standard Hindi.

### 6. Child Playing Outside

English: The child is playing outside.  
Natural Bhojpuri: लइका बाहर खेलत बा।  
Literal / unnatural: बच्चा बाहर खेल रहा है।  
Evaluation note: "लइका" and "खेलत बा" are natural Bhojpuri forms. The literal version is accurate in meaning but not Bhojpuri in grammar or vocabulary.

### 7. Register and Tone

English: This answer sounds too formal.  
Natural Bhojpuri: ई जवाब बहुत बेसी औपचारिक लागत बा।  
Literal / unnatural: यह उत्तर अत्यधिक औपचारिक प्रतीत होता है।  
Evaluation note: The natural version keeps the meaning but lowers the register to conversational Bhojpuri. The literal version is too Sanskritized and formal for most AI assistant responses in Bhojpuri.

### 8. Translation Guideline

English: Do not translate word for word; keep the meaning and tone.  
Natural Bhojpuri: शब्द-शब्द अनुवाद मत करीं; मतलब आ लहजा बचा के रखीं।  
Literal / unnatural: शब्द के लिए शब्द अनुवाद मत करें; अर्थ और टोन रखें।  
Evaluation note: The natural version gives a clear instruction in Bhojpuri while preserving "meaning and tone." The literal version is awkward because it follows English/Hindi structure too closely.

### 9. AI Review Workflow

English: We will check the quality and give feedback.  
Natural Bhojpuri: हमनी क्वालिटी जांचब आ फीडबैक देब।  
Literal / unnatural: हम गुणवत्ता की जांच करेंगे और प्रतिक्रिया देंगे।  
Evaluation note: In modern technical contexts, Bhojpuri speakers may naturally code-switch with words like "क्वालिटी" and "फीडबैक." The literal version sounds formal Hindi rather than natural Bhojpuri.

### 10. Past Event

English: He went to the market but came back early.  
Natural Bhojpuri: ऊ बजार गइल रहे, बाकिर जल्दी लवट आइल।  
Literal / unnatural: वह बाजार गया था लेकिन जल्दी वापस आया।  
Evaluation note: "ऊ," "बजार," "गइल रहे," "बाकिर," and "लवट आइल" create a natural Bhojpuri sentence. The literal version is semantically correct but not Bhojpuri.

## Summary

This sample shows the ability to evaluate Bhojpuri AI output beyond literal meaning. Key review signals include natural verb forms, Bhojpuri pronouns, Hindi influence, register, code-switching, idiomatic phrasing, and cultural fit.
